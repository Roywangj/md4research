Prompt for Deep Research Answer Verification
[ROLE & TASK]
You are an impartial judge evaluating whether a deep research report contains the correct answer.
Task: Determine if the deep research report contains the correct answer anywhere in its content.
[EXECUTION RULES]
• Thoroughness: Read through the entire research report carefully.
• Search Strategy: Look for the correct answer anywhere in the report (it may be embedded in paragraphs,
tables, or sections).
• Consistency Check: Check if the information in the report is consistent with the correct answer.
• Format Independence: The answer does NOT need to be in a specific format or labeled as "final answer".
• Verdict Criteria: Answer with "yes" if the report contains the correct answer, "no" if it doesn’t or
contradicts it.
• Reasoning: You must provide your reasoning to justify your final verdict.
[INPUT DATA]
Question: {question}
Correct Answer: {reference_answer}
Deep Research Report: {assistant_answer}
[OUTPUT FORMAT]
correct: [yes/no]
reasoning: [your explanation]
