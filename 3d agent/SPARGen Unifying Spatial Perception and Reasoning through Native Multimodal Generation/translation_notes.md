# Translation notes

- Source format: selectable-text PDF (`pdf-text`), 9 pages.
- The reader is rebuilt from the PDF in source order and includes bilingual blocks through page 9, including the reference list.
- Tables and figure insets are retained as searchable transcription; PNG crops in `assets/` are visual aids and may include approximate page-region boundaries.
- PDF text extraction joins some multi-column lines; where a symbol or line break is ambiguous, the searchable English transcription is retained and the translation follows the extracted reading order.
