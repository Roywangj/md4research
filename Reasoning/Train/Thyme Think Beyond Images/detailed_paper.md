# Abstract

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 1:</strong></span> Following OpenAI’s introduction of the “thinking with images” concept, recent efforts have explored stimulating the use of visual information in the reasoning process to enhance model performance in perception and reasoning tasks. However, to the best of our knowledge, no open-source work currently offers a feature set as rich as proprietary models (OpenAI O3 (OpenAI, 2025)), which can perform diverse image manipulations and simultaneously enhance logical reasoning capabilities through code.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 1[CN]:</strong></span> 继 OpenAI 提出“用图像思考”（thinking with images）概念之后，近期研究开始探索如何激发模型在推理过程中使用视觉信息，以提升其在感知与推理任务中的性能。然而，据我们所知，目前尚无开源工作能提供与专有模型（OpenAI O3 (OpenAI, 2025)）同样丰富的功能集；后者既能执行多种图像操作，又能通过代码同时增强逻辑推理能力。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 2:</strong></span> In this paper, we make a preliminary attempt in this direction by introducing Thyme (Think Beyond Images), a novel paradigm for enabling multimodal large language models to transcend existing “think with images” approaches by autonomously generating and executing diverse image processing and computational operations via executable code (Figure 2). This approach not only facilitates a rich, on-the-fly set of image manipulations (e.g., cropping, rotation, contrast enhancement), but also allows for mathematical computations, all while maintaining high autonomy in deciding when and how to apply these operations. We activate this capability through a two-stage training strategy: an initial Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on a curated dataset of 500K samples to teach code generation, followed by a Reinforcement Learning (RL) phase to refine decision-making. For the RL stage, we manually collect and design high-resolution question-answer pairs to increase the learning difficulty, and we propose GRPO-ATS (Group Relative Policy Optimization with Adaptive Temperature Sampling), an algorithm that applies distinct temperatures to text and code generation to balance reasoning exploration with code execution precision. We conduct extensive experimental analysis and ablation studies. As shown in Figure 1, comprehensive evaluations on nearly 20 benchmarks show that Thyme yields significant and consistent performance gains, particularly in challenging high-resolution perception and complex reasoning tasks. We release our datasets, sandbox, and code to facilitate future research.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 2[CN]:</strong></span> 在本文中，我们朝这一方向进行了初步尝试，提出 Thyme（Think Beyond Images）：一种新范式，使多模态大语言模型能够通过可执行代码自主生成并执行多种图像处理与计算操作，从而超越现有的“用图像思考”方法（Figure 2）。该方法不仅支持丰富的即时图像操作（例如裁剪、旋转和对比度增强），还支持数学计算，同时在决定何时以及如何应用这些操作方面保持高度自主性。我们通过两阶段训练策略激活这一能力：首先在精心构建的 500K 样本数据集上进行监督微调（Supervised Fine-Tuning, SFT），以教授代码生成；随后进入强化学习（Reinforcement Learning, RL）阶段，以优化决策能力。在 RL 阶段，我们人工收集并设计高分辨率问答对以提高学习难度，并提出 GRPO-ATS（Group Relative Policy Optimization with Adaptive Temperature Sampling），该算法对文本生成与代码生成采用不同温度，从而在推理探索与代码执行精度之间取得平衡。我们开展了广泛的实验分析与消融研究。如 Figure 1 所示，在近 20 个基准上的综合评估表明，Thyme 带来了显著且稳定的性能提升，尤其是在具有挑战性的高分辨率感知和复杂推理任务上。我们公开数据集、沙箱和代码，以促进未来研究。

# 1 Introduction

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 1:</strong></span> Recent years have witnessed considerable interest in enabling multimodal large language models (MLLMs) (Bai et al., 2025a; Team et al., 2025; Shen et al., 2025a; Xiaomi, 2025; Team, 2025; Chen et al., 2023; Fu et al., 2025) to "think with images"—that is, to incorporate visual inputs as part of their reasoning process. Existing approaches to realize this capability generally fall into two categories. One category involves thinking with generated images methods (Chern et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025a; Wu et al., 2025), in which the model first generates an image to guide subsequent reasoning. This approach offers significant advantages in scenarios requiring imagination or auxiliary visual cues, such as drawing auxiliary lines to solve mathematical problems. However, generated images often suffer from quality limitations that hinder the preservation of fine-grained original details, thus constraining improvements in core visual perception tasks. Moreover, the substantial computational cost associated with image generation results in considerable inference latency, which undermines real-time applicability. The other category focuses on thinking with cropping, in which the model is trained to select relevant image regions (e.g., by outputting bounding boxes (Shen et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025b; Huang et al., 2025a; Shao et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2025b; Zheng et al., 2025; Team et al., 2025)), which are then cropped and processed externally. While this strategy enhances perception accuracy and mitigates hallucination, its functionality remains relatively limited. Specifically, it can only perform cropping through bounding box outputs, falling short of the multifunctionality demonstrated by OpenAI’s O3 (OpenAI, 2025), which supports a broader range of operations such as rotation, contrast enhancement, and even coding.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 1[CN]:</strong></span> 近年来，如何让多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）(Bai et al., 2025a; Team et al., 2025; Shen et al., 2025a; Xiaomi, 2025; Team, 2025; Chen et al., 2023; Fu et al., 2025)“用图像思考”——即把视觉输入纳入其推理过程——引起了广泛关注。现有实现这一能力的方法通常分为两类。第一类是使用生成图像进行思考的方法 (Chern et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025a; Wu et al., 2025)，其中模型首先生成一幅图像来引导后续推理。该方法在需要想象力或辅助视觉线索的场景中具有显著优势，例如通过绘制辅助线来解决数学问题。然而，生成图像往往受到质量限制，难以保留细粒度的原始细节，因而限制了核心视觉感知任务的改进。此外，图像生成带来的巨大计算成本会造成显著的推理延迟，从而削弱其实时适用性。第二类侧重于通过裁剪进行思考，即训练模型选择相关图像区域（例如输出边界框 (Shen et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025b; Huang et al., 2025a; Shao et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2025b; Zheng et al., 2025; Team et al., 2025)），随后在外部对这些区域进行裁剪和处理。虽然该策略提高了感知准确率并缓解了幻觉，但其功能仍相对有限。具体而言，它只能通过边界框输出执行裁剪，尚未达到 OpenAI O3 (OpenAI, 2025) 所展现的多功能性；后者支持旋转、对比度增强乃至编码等更广泛的操作。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 2:</strong></span> To overcome these challenges, we propose a novel paradigm called Think Beyond Images (Thyme), illustrated in Figure 2, guided by four core principles:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 2[CN]:</strong></span> 为克服这些挑战，我们提出一种名为 Think Beyond Images（Thyme）的新范式，如 Figure 2 所示，其遵循四项核心原则：

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 3:</strong></span> ⋄ **Rich Functionality:** Enables the model to support a wide range of common image operations such as cropping, scaling, rotating, and contrast enhancement, as well as complex mathematical computations.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 3[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **丰富功能：** 使模型能够支持广泛的常见图像操作，例如裁剪、缩放、旋转和对比度增强，以及复杂的数学计算。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 4:</strong></span> ⋄ **High Autonomy:** Thyme exhibits high autonomy, capable of deciding whether to perform image operations, determining what operations to apply, and executing the chosen functions by dynamically generating code — all without human intervention for specific tasks.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 4[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **高度自主：** Thyme 展现出高度自主性，能够决定是否执行图像操作、确定应用哪些操作，并通过动态生成代码来执行选定功能——整个过程不需要针对具体任务进行人工干预。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 5:</strong></span> ⋄ **Efficient End-to-End Training:** We utilize an end-to-end supervised finetuning + reinforcement learning (SFT+RL) algorithm to minimize training costs and rapidly unlock diverse model capabilities. Notably, the SFT stage requires only 200 GPU hours to activate all the functionalities mentioned above, while the RL stage further strengthens these abilities.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 5[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **高效的端到端训练：** 我们采用端到端监督微调 + 强化学习（SFT+RL）算法，以最大限度降低训练成本并快速解锁模型的多种能力。值得注意的是，SFT 阶段仅需 200 GPU hours 即可激活上述全部功能，而 RL 阶段会进一步强化这些能力。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 6:</strong></span> ⋄ **Significant and Stable Performance Gains:** We evaluate nearly 20 benchmarks spanning three major categories: perception, reasoning, and general tasks. Under the Thyme paradigm, the model achieves consistent and substantial performance improvements across all these task categories.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 6[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **显著且稳定的性能增益：** 我们评估了近 20 个基准，涵盖感知、推理和通用任务三大类别。在 Thyme 范式下，模型在所有这些任务类别上都取得了稳定且显著的性能提升。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 7:</strong></span> To realize this vision, our technical roadmap comprises the following key components:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 7[CN]:</strong></span> 为实现这一愿景，我们的技术路线包含以下关键组成部分：

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 8:</strong></span> ⋄ **SFT Data Construction:** Leveraging over 4 million raw data sources, we curate a high-quality SFT dataset of approximately 500K samples encompassing diverse scenarios: (a) image operations and computations requiring no coding; (b) cropping of complex or high-resolution images; (c) correction of images rotated by large angles; (d) enhancement of low-contrast images; (e) tasks involving complex code-based computations to boost understanding; and (f) multi-turn interactive scenarios, such as iterative magnification and cropping. This rich and varied dataset expedites the model’s acquisition of multimodal capabilities.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 8[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **SFT 数据构建：** 利用超过 400 万条原始数据源，我们筛选并构建了一个约含 500K 样本的高质量 SFT 数据集，覆盖多种场景：(a) 无需编码的图像操作与计算；(b) 对复杂或高分辨率图像进行裁剪；(c) 校正大角度旋转的图像；(d) 增强低对比度图像；(e) 涉及复杂代码计算、旨在提升理解能力的任务；以及 (f) 多轮交互场景，例如迭代放大与裁剪。这个丰富多样的数据集加速了模型对多模态能力的习得。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 9:</strong></span> ⋄ **SFT Training Strategy:** To accommodate the diversity of functionality, we implement specific training schemes. Specifically, outputs and environment feedback from the sandbox are masked during loss computation to prevent gradient contamination; only the model’s last-round response in multi-turn dialogues is used for loss calculation; and annealing techniques are applied on long-tail complex computation samples to thoroughly activate the model’s Thyme capabilities.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 9[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **SFT 训练策略：** 为适应功能的多样性，我们实施了专门的训练方案。具体而言，在计算损失时屏蔽沙箱输出和环境反馈，以防止梯度污染；多轮对话中仅使用模型最后一轮的响应计算损失；并在长尾复杂计算样本上采用退火技术，以充分激活模型的 Thyme 能力。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 10:</strong></span> ⋄ **RL Data Construction.** Our RL dataset primarily consists of two parts. The first part involves filtering open-source data, which contains a mixture of perception and reasoning tasks. However, the image resolution and complexity in public datasets are limited. Therefore, we manually collect and annotate 10,000 high-resolution and perceptually challenging complex perception images. These images are carefully curated for high resolution and complexity, focusing on enhancing the model’s perceptual capabilities and addressing more difficult tasks.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 10[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **RL 数据构建。** 我们的 RL 数据集主要由两部分组成。第一部分通过筛选开源数据获得，其中混合了感知和推理任务。然而，公开数据集的图像分辨率和复杂度有限。因此，我们人工收集并标注了 10,000 幅高分辨率且在感知上具有挑战性的复杂感知图像。这些图像经过精心筛选，兼具高分辨率和高复杂度，重点用于增强模型的感知能力并处理更困难的任务。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 11:</strong></span> ⋄ **RL Algorithm Design.** During the reinforcement learning phase, we observe that using a high sampling temperature for code generation leads to low code usability—sampling any invalid characters or spaces when generating variable names will cause the entire code snippet to fail execution. To mitigate this, we propose GRPO with Adaptive Temperature Sampling (GRPO-ATS ), which applies different temperatures for text and code generation. Specifically, a temperature of 1.0 encourages exploration during text generation, while a temperature of 0.0 minimizes randomness during code generation, thereby ensuring code validity and reducing runtime errors.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 11[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **RL 算法设计。** 在强化学习阶段，我们观察到，代码生成采用较高采样温度会导致代码可用性降低——在生成变量名时采样到任何无效字符或空格，都会使整段代码执行失败。为缓解这一问题，我们提出采用自适应温度采样的 GRPO（GRPO-ATS ），对文本生成与代码生成应用不同温度。具体而言，温度 1.0 鼓励文本生成过程中的探索，而温度 0.0 最大限度减少代码生成过程中的随机性，从而确保代码有效并减少运行时错误。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 12:</strong></span> ⋄ **Sandbox Design.** We develop a secure sandbox environment capable of executing model-generated code within strict time limits and returning results. This environment minimizes the model’s code burden by automatically handling formatting, variable definitions, and boundary conditions related to image operations, significantly improving code usability without altering its semantic intent.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 12[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **沙箱设计。** 我们开发了一个安全的沙箱环境，能够在严格的时间限制内执行模型生成的代码并返回结果。该环境通过自动处理格式、变量定义和与图像操作相关的边界条件来减轻模型的编码负担，在不改变代码语义意图的前提下显著提高代码可用性。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 13:</strong></span> Thyme effectively fulfills our vision by combining high autonomy with rich functionality. It can assess the complexity of a given problem and determine whether tool usage is necessary. When required, it autonomously defines and invokes tools through code, even performing multiple operations such as cropping, zooming, and rotating in a single execution. Moreover, the SFT training phase requires only about 200 GPU hours to activate the model’s fundamental abilities to perform various image manipulations and computations, reflecting favorable computational efficiency. We conduct comprehensive evaluations of Thyme across more than 20 multimodal benchmarks. Experimental results in Figure 1 demonstrate that Thyme exhibits significant and consistent advantages in fundamental perception, complex reasoning, and computation tasks. Furthermore, the complete dataset, sandbox environment, and training code will be open-sourced to facilitate further community research and adoption.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 13[CN]:</strong></span> Thyme 将高度自主性与丰富功能相结合，有效实现了我们的愿景。它能够评估给定问题的复杂度，并判断是否有必要使用工具。需要时，它会通过代码自主定义并调用工具，甚至在一次执行中完成裁剪、缩放和旋转等多种操作。此外，SFT 训练阶段仅需约 200 GPU hours，即可激活模型执行各种图像操作与计算的基本能力，体现出良好的计算效率。我们在 20 多个多模态基准上对 Thyme 进行了全面评估。Figure 1 中的实验结果表明，Thyme 在基础感知、复杂推理和计算任务上展现出显著且稳定的优势。此外，我们将开源完整的数据集、沙箱环境和训练代码，以促进社区进一步研究与采用。

# 2 Preliminary and Overall Pipeline

## 2.1 Overal Pipeline of Thyme

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 1:</strong></span> As shown in Figure 2, the overall pipeline mainly consists of two components: the model and the sandbox. Given a user input, the model first generates a reasoning process, which includes analyzing the problem and deciding, based on the type and difficulty of the problem, whether to generate code.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 1[CN]:</strong></span> 如 Figure 2 所示，整体流程主要由两个组成部分构成：模型与沙箱。给定用户输入后，模型首先生成推理过程，其中包括分析问题，并根据问题的类型与难度决定是否生成代码。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 2:</strong></span> If no code generation is required, this implies the problem is simple, or that through previous dialogue rounds, relevant code operations have already successfully solved the problem. In such cases, the answer is returned directly. If code generation is needed, the model autonomously produces the code. Our training data covers several types of image operations such as cropping, zooming, rotation, contrast enhancement, and computations. The model may implement one or a combination of these functions in the generated code, for example performing cropping, zooming, and contrast enhancement simultaneously. The input parameters of these operations, such as the contrast enhancement factor, crop coordinates, and zoom ratio, are all decided by the model itself with no external constraints.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 2[CN]:</strong></span> 如果不需要生成代码，这意味着问题较简单，或者相关代码操作已在之前的对话轮次中成功解决了该问题。在这种情况下，模型直接返回答案。如果需要生成代码，模型会自主生成代码。我们的训练数据覆盖多种图像操作，例如裁剪、缩放、旋转、对比度增强和计算。模型可以在所生成的代码中实现其中一种功能或多种功能的组合，例如同时执行裁剪、缩放和对比度增强。这些操作的输入参数，例如对比度增强因子、裁剪坐标和缩放比例，都由模型自行决定，不受外部约束。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 3:</strong></span> The generated code is then executed within an external sandbox, whose main function is to securely handle the input code and return execution results. Inside the sandbox, we utilize Python’s built-in modules such as ast, autopep8, etc., to perform code formatting, correct input-output variable properties, and fix code input parameters. This is done without affecting the code functionality but to avoid errors caused by trivial code details, thereby reducing the model’s code burden and improving code usability.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 3[CN]:</strong></span> 随后，生成的代码会在外部沙箱中执行；沙箱的主要功能是安全处理输入代码并返回执行结果。在沙箱内部，我们利用 Python 的 ast、autopep8 等内置模块进行代码格式化、修正输入输出变量属性并修复代码输入参数。这些处理不会影响代码功能，而是用于避免由琐碎代码细节引起的错误，从而减轻模型的编码负担并提高代码可用性。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 4:</strong></span> Finally, the code execution results are returned to the model for the next dialogue round, and the model conducts reasoning based on these results to continue the interaction.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 4[CN]:</strong></span> 最后，代码执行结果会返回给模型，用于下一轮对话；模型基于这些结果进行推理并继续交互。

**Caption:** Figure 3: SFT Data Construction Pipeline. First, samples are taken from an existing dataset and prompts are constructed based on the target functions (such as cropping, rotating, etc.). The model generates a thinking process and corresponding code based on the prompt. The code is then executed in a sandbox environment to filter out samples that fail to run properly. The remaining samples are reviewed by an additional MLLM to verify whether the code execution results align with the thinking process and effectively answer the question, eliminating invalid code samples. Finally, manual review is conducted to remove low-quality samples, ensuring the quality of the cold-start dataset.

**Caption[CN]:** Figure 3：SFT 数据构建流程。首先，从现有数据集中抽取样本，并根据目标功能（例如裁剪、旋转等）构建提示。模型基于提示生成思考过程及相应代码。随后在沙箱环境中执行代码，以筛除无法正常运行的样本。再由另一个 MLLM 审核剩余样本，验证代码执行结果是否与思考过程一致并能有效回答问题，从而剔除无效代码样本。最后，通过人工审核移除低质量样本，以确保冷启动数据集的质量。

## 2.2 Sandbox Building

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 5:</strong></span> Our framework requires a robust sandbox with two essential properties. First, it ensures basic security and robustness for the operating system, guaranteeing that any image processing or computational operations executed within the sandbox complete within a reasonable timeframe or throw appropriate errors. At the same time, it prevents unauthorized system modifications, such as file deletion or renaming, which could corrupt existing data or runtime configurations.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 5[CN]:</strong></span> 我们的框架需要一个具备两项关键属性的稳健沙箱。首先，它要保证操作系统的基本安全性与稳健性，确保沙箱内执行的任何图像处理或计算操作都能在合理时间内完成，或者抛出适当错误。同时，它还要防止未经授权的系统修改，例如删除或重命名文件，因为这些操作可能破坏现有数据或运行时配置。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 6:</strong></span> We observe that smaller models (e.g., 7B parameters) often struggle to generate flawless code, frequently encountering minor issues such as formatting problems (improper indentation), boundary condition errors (cropping boxes exceeding image dimensions), and input/output handling (omitting input variables or output printing). Therefore, the sandbox design aims to reduce the model’s coding burden by automatically handling these minor issues when possible.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 6[CN]:</strong></span> 我们观察到，较小的模型（例如 7B 参数）往往难以生成毫无缺陷的代码，经常遇到格式问题（缩进不当）、边界条件错误（裁剪框超出图像尺寸）和输入/输出处理问题（遗漏输入变量或输出打印）等小问题。因此，沙箱设计旨在尽可能自动处理这些小问题，从而减轻模型的编码负担。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 7:</strong></span> To address sandbox security and robustness, we apply the following strategies:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 7[CN]:</strong></span> 为解决沙箱安全性与稳健性问题，我们采用以下策略：

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 8:</strong></span> ⋄ We define a list of dangerous operations—such as remove, unlink, move, and rename—that may cause system data or configuration failures. Before execution, the code is scanned for these operations; if it contains any, execution skips and a warning raises as an error.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 8[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ 我们定义了一份危险操作列表，其中包括 remove、unlink、move 和 rename 等可能造成系统数据或配置故障的操作。执行前会扫描代码是否包含这些操作；如果包含，则跳过执行，并将警告作为错误抛出。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 9:</strong></span> ⋄ We limit the maximum execution time, empirically set to 10 seconds. Exceeding this limit results in a timeout error. These measures help ensure code isolates from the local system and runs safely.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 9[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ 我们限制最长执行时间，并根据经验将其设为 10 秒。超过该限制会产生超时错误。这些措施有助于确保代码与本地系统隔离并安全运行。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 10:</strong></span> To reduce the model’s code generation burden, we implement the following optimizations:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 10[CN]:</strong></span> 为减轻模型的代码生成负担，我们实施了以下优化：

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 11:</strong></span> ⋄ We apply Python’s built-in autopep8 for code formatting and indentation alignment.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 11[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ 我们使用 Python 内置的 autopep8 进行代码格式化与缩进对齐。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 12:</strong></span> ⋄ Using the ast module, we traverse the code to identify variables in tuple formats. If variable names contain box or coord, we infer they represent cropping parameters; these parameters adjust to fit within the input image boundaries to avoid execution errors.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 12[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ 我们使用 ast 模块遍历代码，以识别元组格式的变量。如果变量名包含 box 或 coord，我们便推断它们表示裁剪参数；这些参数会被调整到输入图像边界之内，以避免执行错误。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 13:</strong></span> ⋄ Before execution, we preset local variables such as image_path and import packages like cv2. After execution, we check for newly created variables indicative of processed outputs (e.g., those containing ‘processed_path’) to ensure correct execution even if input/output variables do not explicitly define in the model-generated code.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 13[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ 执行前，我们预设 image_path 等局部变量，并导入 cv2 等软件包。执行后，我们检查是否存在表示处理输出的新建变量（例如名称中包含 ‘processed_path’ 的变量），从而确保即使模型生成的代码没有显式定义输入/输出变量，也能正确执行。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 14:</strong></span> ⋄ Additionally, we observe that when the model outputs multiple code segments, context or variable dependencies often arise; for example, certain Python packages are imported only in the first segment, while subsequent segments rely on them directly. To address this, we record all variables throughout the model’s code execution process and incorporate historical context in multi-round sandbox invocations.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 14[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ 此外，我们观察到，当模型输出多个代码段时，往往会产生上下文依赖或变量依赖；例如，某些 Python 软件包仅在第一个代码段中导入，而后续代码段会直接依赖它们。为解决这一问题，我们记录模型代码执行过程中的所有变量，并在多轮沙箱调用中纳入历史上下文。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 4:</strong></span> **Real-World Thyme Data:** Moving towards increased complexity, samples requiring code-based operations undergo rigorous verification and quality control. Initially, all code snippets pass through our sandbox environment where non-executable codes are discarded to ensure only valid samples proceed. Subsequently, the execution outputs, alongside the model’s own analysis and answers, feed back into Qwen2.5-VL-72B to verify whether the intended goals—such as clearer identification of the target region by cropping or enhanced readability through rotation—are effectively achieved. The model flags and eliminates any samples where code does not fulfill those expectations. To further enhance data reliability, two multimodal experts perform an additional round of meticulous screening, filtering out ambiguous or borderline cases beyond the model’s discernment. These samples reflect realistic and complex visual-question answering scenarios drawn from more than 4 million instances across public datasets, culminating in a refined collection of 50k high-quality, executable QA pairs.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 4[CN]:</strong></span> **真实世界 Thyme 数据：** 随着复杂度提高，需要基于代码操作的样本会经历严格的验证与质量控制。首先，所有代码片段都要通过我们的沙箱环境，其中不可执行的代码会被丢弃，以确保只有有效样本进入后续流程。随后，执行输出连同模型自身的分析与答案会反馈给 Qwen2.5-VL-72B，以验证预期目标是否得到有效实现，例如通过裁剪更清晰地识别目标区域，或通过旋转提高可读性。模型会标记并剔除代码未达到这些预期的样本。为进一步提高数据可靠性，两位多模态专家会再进行一轮细致筛选，过滤模型无法辨别的模糊或边界案例。这些样本来自公开数据集中的 400 多万个实例，反映真实而复杂的视觉问答场景，最终得到一套精炼的 50k 高质量、可执行 QA 对。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 5:</strong></span> **Manually Constructed Thyme Data:** Given that real-world Thyme data often have high complexity and quality but limited quantity—we complement this by deliberately creating an additional manually constructed dataset[^1]. This curated data supplements and enriches the training distribution, helping the model perform robustly across various task types. The verification and filtering protocols for this synthetic dataset parallel those used for real-world data, ensuring consistent quality standards.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 5[CN]:</strong></span> **人工构建的 Thyme 数据：** 鉴于真实世界 Thyme 数据通常复杂度和质量较高但数量有限，我们通过有意创建额外的人工构建数据集[^1]来加以补充。这些精心构建的数据补充并丰富了训练分布，帮助模型在各种任务类型上保持稳健表现。该合成数据集的验证与筛选流程与真实世界数据所采用的流程一致，从而确保统一的质量标准。

[^1]: **Footnote 1:** Most real-world data features high-resolution images or complex problems where additional image processing significantly improves perceptual effectiveness. In contrast, manually constructed data often contains smaller resolution images (e.g., V*), where using prompts from Table 7 might cause the model to simply output answers, necessitating specialized construction procedures.  
    **Footnote 1[CN]:** 大多数真实世界数据包含高分辨率图像或复杂问题，在这些场景中，额外的图像处理可显著提升感知效果。相比之下，人工构建的数据通常包含分辨率较低的图像（例如 V*）；在此类数据上使用 Table 7 中的提示可能会使模型直接输出答案，因此需要专门的构建流程。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 6:</strong></span> ⋄ **Coding Data 1: Cropping** — This dataset draws primarily from V*, which features questions paired with images, corresponding answers, and ground truth bounding box coordinates for target objects. By applying the prompt outlined in Table 8, the model is prompted to explain the rationale behind cropping the object and its contribution to problem-solving. Cropping then proceeds based on the ground truth coordinates before providing answers. Despite leveraging accurate bounding box information, code generation quality constraints restrict the final dataset size to 28k samples.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 6[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **编码数据 1：裁剪** ——该数据集主要来自 V*，其中包含与图像配对的问题、相应答案以及目标对象的真实边界框坐标。通过使用 Table 8 所示提示，引导模型解释裁剪该对象的理由，以及裁剪对解决问题的贡献。随后，先根据真实坐标进行裁剪，再给出答案。尽管利用了准确的边界框信息，但代码生成质量方面的限制使最终数据集规模仅为 28k 个样本。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 7:</strong></span> ⋄ **Coding Data 2: Rotation** — Addressing the scarcity of rotated image samples in conventional training datasets, we augment the data by randomly rotating images within the collected pool at angles ranging from 30° to 335°. Applying the prompt template in Table 9, the model generates appropriate processing code and accompanying analytical commentary informed by ground truth rotation angles. Notably, we observe the relative insensitivity of existing MLLMs to rotation angles; that is, without ground truth inputs, the majority of samples cannot be reoriented correctly. After careful filtering, this yields a set of 14k high-fidelity samples.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 7[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **编码数据 2：旋转** ——针对常规训练数据集中旋转图像样本稀缺的问题，我们对所收集样本池中的图像进行随机旋转，旋转角度范围为 30° 至 335°，以增强数据。使用 Table 9 中的提示模板后，模型依据真实旋转角度生成适当的处理代码及配套分析说明。值得注意的是，我们观察到现有 MLLMs 对旋转角度相对不敏感；也就是说，如果没有真实值输入，大多数样本都无法被正确重新定向。经过仔细筛选后，最终得到一组 14k 高保真样本。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 8:</strong></span> ⋄ **Coding Data 3: Low-Contrast Enhancement** — Derived mainly from OCR data within LLaVA-OV-Image, this subset targets scenarios in which contrast enhancement reduces the difficulty of text recognition, thereby aiding the model’s text extraction capabilities. We repurpose prompts akin to those in Table 8, swapping out cropping directives for contrast enhancement instructions. Qwen2.5-VL-72B generates explanations of the enhancement’s purpose, corresponding code, and final visual results. The curated dataset contains approximately 10k high-quality training examples.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 8[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **编码数据 3：低对比度增强** ——该子集主要源自 LLaVA-OV-Image 中的 OCR 数据，针对对比度增强可以降低文本识别难度、进而帮助模型提升文本提取能力的场景。我们复用与 Table 8 类似的提示，将其中的裁剪指令替换为对比度增强指令。Qwen2.5-VL-72B 生成对增强目的的解释、相应代码和最终视觉结果。经过筛选的数据集包含约 10k 个高质量训练样本。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 9:</strong></span> ⋄ **Coding Data 4: Computational Code** — To not only support image manipulation but also foster enhanced logical reasoning through mathematical computation, we construct a specialized math-centric dataset. Its principal source is MM-Eureka and Retool. Initial long-chain-of-thought solutions are generated by Gemini 2.5 Pro, which are then transformed by GPT-4o into executable code segments.[^2] This process results in 15k samples focused on computational reasoning via code.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 9[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **编码数据 4：计算代码** ——为不仅支持图像操作，还能通过数学计算促进逻辑推理能力提升，我们构建了一个专门的、以数学为中心的数据集。其主要来源为 MM-Eureka 和 Retool。初始的长思维链解答由 Gemini 2.5 Pro 生成，随后由 GPT-4o 将其转换为可执行代码段。[^2] 这一过程最终产生 15k 个聚焦于通过代码进行计算推理的样本。

[^2]: **Footnote 2:** Although Gemini 2.5 Pro produces detailed and comprehensive solutions, its code tends to be overly complex and heavily annotated, making it less suitable for model training. Therefore, GPT-4o handles code conversion to produce leaner implementations.  
    **Footnote 2[CN]:** 尽管 Gemini 2.5 Pro 能生成详细而全面的解答，但其代码往往过于复杂且包含大量注释，因此不太适合模型训练。故由 GPT-4o 负责代码转换，以生成更精简的实现。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 10:</strong></span> **Multi-Round Conversation:** Except for the Computational Code data, all the aforementioned datasets involve only a single round of code generation and execution. Our experiments reveal that models trained solely on single-turn interactions lack error-correction capabilities. Specifically, even if the model incorrectly crops a sub-region, it proceeds to generate an answer based on the flawed crop without attempting to re-crop or further adjust the targeted area. To address this limitation, we manually construct multi-round conversational datasets aimed at teaching the model to leverage historical code execution outcomes for deeper analysis and error rectification. This multi-round data is categorized into two types:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 10[CN]:</strong></span> **多轮对话：** 除 Computational Code 数据外，上述所有数据集都只涉及一轮代码生成与执行。我们的实验表明，仅在单轮交互上训练的模型缺乏纠错能力。具体而言，即使模型错误裁剪了一个子区域，它仍会基于有缺陷的裁剪结果生成答案，而不会尝试重新裁剪或进一步调整目标区域。为解决这一限制，我们人工构建了多轮对话数据集，旨在教会模型利用历史代码执行结果开展更深入的分析与错误修正。这些多轮数据分为两类：

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 11:</strong></span> ⋄ **Further Enhancement:** The model refines or augments previous results based on feedback. We sample data from V* and utilize the prompt shown in Table 8, but initially provide a larger bounding box that fully contains the ground truth cropping box. Based on the code execution results from this first round, the model generates further analysis and code to crop precisely to the ground truth bounding box.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 11[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **进一步增强：** 模型根据反馈优化或增强先前结果。我们从 V* 中抽取数据并使用 Table 8 所示提示，但最初提供一个完全包含真实裁剪框的较大边界框。模型基于第一轮的代码执行结果生成进一步分析和代码，以精确裁剪至真实边界框。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 12:</strong></span> ⋄ **Error Correction:** The model detects and fixes mistakes identified in earlier steps. Similarly, we provide an incorrect bounding box in the first step. In the second step, the model generates the corrected bounding box with an explanation on why the cropping region needs adjustment.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 12[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **错误纠正：** 模型检测并修复在较早步骤中发现的错误。类似地，我们在第一步提供一个不正确的边界框。第二步中，模型生成纠正后的边界框，并解释为什么需要调整裁剪区域。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 18:</strong></span> To address these issues, we devise several training-phase strategies:

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 19:</strong></span> ⋄ **Train on the Last Round Only:** For samples with two or more rounds, mask out all content before and including the last `<sandbox_output></sandbox_output>` tag, such that the model learns only to generate outputs corresponding to the final round. > <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 19[CN]:</strong></span> ⋄ **仅在最后一轮上训练：** 对于包含两轮或更多轮次的样本，屏蔽最后一个 `<sandbox_output></sandbox_output>` 标签之前以及包括该标签在内的所有内容，使模型只学习生成与最后一轮相对应的输出。换言之，所有 $[T_{t-1}, C_{t-1}, S_{t-1}]$ 都会被屏蔽，只学习最后一轮的逻辑与结果。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 20:</strong></span> ⋄ **Sandbox Content Exclusion:** Throughout all training stages, we exclude the sandbox identifier `<sandbox_output></sandbox_output>` markers and sandbox execution results from the training targets. ### 4.2 Preliminary of Reinforcement Learning Algorithm

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 6:</strong></span> **Training Algorithm:** We adopt a fully on-policy variant of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) (Shao et al., 2024b) algorithm. For each problem consisting of an image $I$ and a question $Q$, the algorithm samples a group of interaction trajectories $\{\tau_1, \tau_2, \ldots, \tau_G\}$ from the policy $\pi_\theta$, where each trajectory $\tau_i$ represents a complete multi-round interaction:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 6[CN]:</strong></span> **训练算法：** 我们采用 Group Relative Policy Optimization（GRPO）算法 (Shao et al., 2024b) 的完全 on-policy 变体。对于每个由图像 $I$ 和问题 $Q$ 构成的问题，该算法从策略 $\pi_\theta$ 中采样一组交互轨迹 $\{\tau_1, \tau_2, \ldots, \tau_G\}$，其中每条轨迹 $\tau_i$ 表示一次完整的多轮交互：

$$
\tau_i = \{(I,Q); ([T_{i,0}, C_{i,0}, S_{i,0}], \ldots, [T_{i,t_i}, a_i])\}
$$

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 7:</strong></span> The policy is updated by maximizing the following objective:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 7[CN]:</strong></span> 通过最大化以下目标来更新策略：

$$
J_{\mathrm{GRPO}}(\theta)
=
\mathbb{E}_{(I,Q)\sim D,\{\tau_i\}_{i=1}^{G}\sim\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid I,Q)}
\left[
\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{G}|\tau_i|}
\sum_{i=1}^{G}\sum_{j=1}^{|\tau_i|} A_{i,j}
- \beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}[\pi_\theta\|\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}]
\right]
$$

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 8:</strong></span> where $|\tau_i|$ is the total number of tokens generated by the model in trajectory $i$, specifically:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 8[CN]:</strong></span> 其中，$|\tau_i|$ 是模型在轨迹 $i$ 中生成的 token 总数，具体为：

$$
|\tau_i| = \sum_{k=0}^{t_i}|T_{i,k}| + \sum_{k=0}^{t_i-1}|C_{i,k}| + |a_i|
$$

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 9:</strong></span> and $A_{i,j}$ is the advantage computed from the final rewards $\{r_1, r_2, \ldots, r_G\}$ of the complete trajectories in the same group:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 9[CN]:</strong></span> 而 $A_{i,j}$ 是根据同一组中完整轨迹的最终奖励 $\{r_1, r_2, \ldots, r_G\}$ 计算得到的优势值：

$$
A_{i,j} = \frac{r_i-\operatorname{mean}(\{r_i\}_{i=1}^{G})}{\operatorname{std}(\{r_i\}_{i=1}^{G})}
\quad \text{for each token } j \text{ in } \tau_i
\tag{2}
$$

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 10:</strong></span> The term $-\beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}[\pi_\theta\|\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}]$ penalizes large deviations of the current policy $\pi_\theta$ from a reference policy $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$, with $\beta$ controlling the strength of the constraint. The reward $r_i$ evaluates the quality of the entire trajectory, including the effectiveness of code generation, sandbox utilization, and the correctness of the final answer $a_i$. Importantly, during the RL stage, the trajectory length $|\tau_i|$ and the summation over tokens in the advantage calculation exclude the sandbox-related content $S_{i,k}$, as these are external observations rather than outputs of the policy model.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 10[CN]:</strong></span> 项 $-\beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}[\pi_\theta\|\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}]$ 对当前策略 $\pi_\theta$ 与参考策略 $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$ 之间的较大偏差施加惩罚，其中 $\beta$ 控制约束强度。奖励 $r_i$ 评估整条轨迹的质量，包括代码生成的有效性、sandbox 的使用情况以及最终答案 $a_i$ 的正确性。重要的是，在 RL 阶段，轨迹长度 $|\tau_i|$ 以及优势计算中对 token 的求和均不包含 sandbox 相关内容 $S_{i,k}$，因为它们是外部观测，而不是策略模型的输出。

### 4.3 GRPO with Adaptive Temperature Sampling

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 11:</strong></span> Our motivation is that code generation and textual reasoning require different sampling parameters. Code generation tasks—such as image manipulation and numerical computation—follow predictable logical sequences. These sequences typically involve loading data, defining parameters, executing operations, and saving results. Such processes require precision rather than exploration. Empirical evidence shows that high temperature settings significantly reduce code reliability. Even minor errors, like an unexpected space character during variable definition, can cause complete code failure. Therefore, we set the temperature to 0 during code generation phases to ensure deterministic output. For natural language reasoning, however, we employ a temperature of 1.0. This setting encourages broader conceptual exploration and diverse expression. As shown in Figure 6, our system implements dynamic temperature adjustment. When the `<code>` token appears, the temperature automatically drops to 0. After code generation completes, the system returns to the default temperature setting for normal text generation. In our experiments, we observe two main benefits:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 11[CN]:</strong></span> 我们的动机在于，代码生成和文本推理需要不同的采样参数。代码生成任务——例如图像操作和数值计算——遵循可预测的逻辑序列。这些序列通常涉及加载数据、定义参数、执行操作和保存结果。这类过程需要精确性而非探索。经验证据表明，高温度设置会显著降低代码的可靠性。即使是微小错误，例如在定义变量时出现意外的空格字符，也可能导致代码完全失效。因此，我们在代码生成阶段将温度设置为 0，以确保确定性输出。然而，对于自然语言推理，我们采用 1.0 的温度。该设置鼓励更广泛的概念探索和更多样的表达。如 Figure 6 所示，我们的系统实现了动态温度调整。当 `<code>` token 出现时，温度会自动降至 0。代码生成完成后，系统恢复默认温度设置以进行正常文本生成。在实验中，我们观察到两个主要收益：

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 12[CN]:</strong></span>
>
> - 它提高了样本效率。在 RL rollout 期间，许多样本会因混乱或错误的代码生成而变得不可用，例如不断尝试错误地生成代码，并持续失败直至耗尽最大交互步数。降低代码温度有助于这些样本恢复并变为有效样本。
> - 它防止模型坍缩为完全回避代码生成。由于编写代码比自然语言推理更容易出错，如果不调整采样参数，训练 rollout 期间生成的大多数代码都是无效的。久而久之，这会导致模型过度保守，并倾向于完全避免编写代码。降低温度并由此提高生成代码的可用性，能够有效缓解这一问题。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 13:</strong></span> **Sampling Optimization:** Another major challenge stems from the multi-turn interactive nature of the task: the maximum number of interaction rounds imposes a strict sampling time budget, as extended or repetitive dialogues significantly increase computational overhead. When the model enters a cycle of repetitive errors, it produces redundant outputs across multiple turns, thereby wasting computational resources on invalid samples. To mitigate this, we implement an early termination mechanism during training. Specifically, we utilize the Rabin-Karp rolling hash algorithm (Leiserson et al., 1994; Karp and Rabin, 1987) to count occurrences of all substrings of fixed length within the model’s output. If any substring repeats so frequently that the cumulative length of its occurrences exceeds 50% of the total output length, we classify the output as repetitive. Once this criterion is met, the sampling process for that particular sample is immediately halted to avoid unnecessary computation. Additionally, during training, we enforce a manual cap on the maximum allowed number of dialogue turns, denoted as max iterations. Dialogues that exceed this limit without terminating are truncated to control context length, thereby improving training efficiency.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 13[CN]:</strong></span> **采样优化：** 另一个主要挑战来自任务的多轮交互性质：最大交互轮数施加了严格的采样时间预算，因为过长或重复的对话会显著增加计算开销。当模型陷入重复错误的循环时，它会在多轮中产生冗余输出，从而把计算资源浪费在无效样本上。为缓解这一问题，我们在训练期间实施了提前终止机制。具体而言，我们使用 Rabin-Karp 滚动哈希算法 (Leiserson et al., 1994; Karp and Rabin, 1987) 统计模型输出中所有固定长度子串的出现次数。如果某个子串重复得如此频繁，以至于其所有出现位置的累计长度超过总输出长度的 50%，我们便将该输出判定为重复。一旦满足这一标准，就立即停止该特定样本的采样过程，以避免不必要的计算。此外，在训练期间，我们还人工设定允许的最大对话轮数上限，记为 max iterations。超过该上限而仍未终止的对话会被截断，以控制上下文长度，从而提高训练效率。

### 4.4 Reward Function Design:

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 14:</strong></span>
>
> - **Formatting Reward:** The model’s output is required to conform to a strict structure, enclosed by `<think></think>` tags followed by `<answer></answer>` tags. This encourages the model to explicitly perform reasoning before producing a final answer, improving the interpretability.
> - **Result Reward:** Since not all targets are strictly numerical or formulaic answers amenable to rule-based comparison, we first apply a rule-based method to directly compare the model output with the ground truth answer. If the rule-based matching fails, we then utilize an auxiliary MLLM, Qwen-2.5-VL-72B, to compare the model-generated output against the ground truth by assessing semantic equivalence and correctness. This hybrid reward approach, combining rule-based and model-based evaluation, improves scoring efficiency and is well-suited for open-ended question answering scenarios.
> - **Consistency Reward** (Team et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025d): This component evaluates whether the final answer is logically derived from the preceding reasoning steps, ensuring coherence between the thought process and the conclusion.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 14[CN]:</strong></span>
>
> - **格式奖励（Formatting Reward）：** 模型输出必须遵循严格结构：先由 `<think></think>` 标签包围，再接由 `<answer></answer>` 标签包围的内容。这鼓励模型在给出最终答案之前显式执行推理，从而提高可解释性。
> - **结果奖励（Result Reward）：** 由于并非所有目标都是适合基于规则比较的严格数值答案或公式化答案，我们首先采用基于规则的方法，直接比较模型输出与真实答案。如果规则匹配失败，则使用辅助 MLLM Qwen-2.5-VL-72B，通过评估语义等价性和正确性，将模型生成的输出与真实答案进行比较。这种结合基于规则与基于模型评估的混合奖励方法提高了评分效率，并且非常适合开放式问答场景。
> - **一致性奖励（Consistency Reward）** (Team et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025d)：该组成部分评估最终答案是否由之前的推理步骤合乎逻辑地推导而来，以确保思维过程与结论之间的一致性。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 15:</strong></span> Integrating the consistency reward alongside formatting and result rewards can cause unintended behavior: the model might receive a high total reward by maintaining consistency even when the chosen answer is incorrect. To prevent this, we define the final reward as follows:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 15[CN]:</strong></span> 将一致性奖励与格式奖励和结果奖励结合起来，可能引发非预期行为：即使所选答案错误，模型也可能通过保持一致性而获得很高的总奖励。为防止这种情况，我们将最终奖励定义如下：

$$
\text{Final Reward } r
= \text{Result Reward} \times \left(1 + 0.5 \times \text{Consistency Reward}\right)
+ 0.5 \times \text{Formatting Reward}.
$$

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 16:</strong></span> This formulation ensures that the consistency bonus is considered only when the model’s answer is correct, avoiding excessive prioritization of consistency at the expense of answer accuracy.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 16[CN]:</strong></span> 该公式确保只有当模型答案正确时才考虑一致性奖励，从而避免以牺牲答案准确性为代价过度优先考虑一致性。

### 5.4 Ablation and Analysis

#### 5.4.1 The Impact of Training Strategies on the SFT Process

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 7:</strong></span> In this subsection, we conduct detailed ablations on different strategies during the SFT phase. The specific results appear in Table 5. Below we introduce and compare these strategies.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 7[CN]:</strong></span> 在本小节中，我们对 SFT 阶段的不同策略进行详细消融。具体结果见 Table 5。下面我们介绍并比较这些策略。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 8:</strong></span>
>
> - **Naive SFT:** This approach directly uses all data for SFT without incorporating any of our training strategies. Direct SFT does not yield significant performance gains, and the model’s output format is quite chaotic. During inference, it tends to predict the content of the sandbox on its own, and the code blocks and sandbox outputs are sometimes overwritten by the model’s own predictions.
> - **Mask Sandbox:** In the SFT stage, we mask sandbox labels and outputs. This requires the model only to learn how to write code and how to perform further reasoning based on the returned results, leading to a significant enhancement in performance.
> - **Only Last Round:** This strategy masks information from previous rounds. For tasks involving multi-round code execution, the model only needs to learn the output of the final round. This effectively prevents the model from learning strange patterns, such as a tendency to produce a suboptimal piece of code in the first round and then correct it in subsequent rounds.
> - **Without Code Comment:** We also investigate the impact of the presence of comments in the code on the model’s performance. In this line of experiments, we remove all comments from the code in the SFT data for training, but we find the results are not satisfactory. We speculate the reason is that while comments do not play a role during code execution, the process of writing comments implies an understanding of the code, making the model more logical when it writes code.
> - **Math Data Annealing:** Since our volume of code data for mathematical calculations is relatively small, when it is mixed with the full dataset for training, the model is rarely observed to write computation-related code during the inference phase. The primary goal of the SFT stage is to enable the model to learn this behavior. Therefore, although annealing this portion of the data does not show significant performance gains compared to the "Only Last Round" strategy, it more effectively introduces the behavior of using code to perform complex calculations.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 8[CN]:</strong></span>
>
> - **Naive SFT：** 该方法直接使用全部数据进行 SFT，而不采用我们的任何训练策略。直接 SFT 并未带来显著性能提升，并且模型的输出格式相当混乱。在推理期间，它倾向于自行预测 sandbox 的内容，代码块和 sandbox 输出有时也会被模型自己的预测覆盖。
> - **Mask Sandbox：** 在 SFT 阶段，我们屏蔽 sandbox 标签和输出。这使模型只需学习如何编写代码，以及如何根据返回结果进一步推理，从而显著提升性能。
> - **Only Last Round：** 该策略屏蔽前几轮的信息。对于涉及多轮代码执行的任务，模型只需学习最后一轮的输出。这可以有效防止模型学到奇怪的模式，例如倾向于在第一轮生成一段次优代码，然后在后续轮次中加以纠正。
> - **Without Code Comment：** 我们还研究了代码中是否存在注释对模型性能的影响。在这组实验中，我们移除了 SFT 训练数据中代码的所有注释，但发现结果并不理想。我们推测其原因在于：虽然注释在代码执行期间不起作用，但编写注释的过程意味着对代码有所理解，从而使模型在编写代码时更具逻辑性。
> - **Math Data Annealing：** 由于我们用于数学计算的代码数据量相对较小，当它与完整数据集混合训练时，在推理阶段很少观察到模型编写与计算相关的代码。SFT 阶段的主要目标是使模型学会这种行为。因此，尽管对这部分数据进行退火训练，相较 "Only Last Round" 策略并未表现出显著性能增益，但它能更有效地引入使用代码执行复杂计算的行为。

#### 5.4.2 The Influence of RL Reward Design on the RL Process

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 9:</strong></span> In this subsection, we explore a variety of reward design strategies, summarize the challenges encountered, and share insights with the aim of informing future work, as shown in Table 6. Besides format rewards and outcome rewards, the main additional reward functions include:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 9[CN]:</strong></span> 在本小节中，我们探索多种奖励设计策略，总结遇到的挑战，并分享见解，以期为未来工作提供参考，如 Table 6 所示。除格式奖励和结果奖励外，主要的附加奖励函数包括：

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 10:</strong></span>
>
> - **Consistency reward:** We provide Qwen2.5-VL-72B with the last 500 characters of the thinking process (usually a summary) and the answer content, asking it to assess the consistency between the reasoning and the final answer.
> - **Code reward:** This reward is the proportion of model-generated code that runs successfully, calculated by dividing the number of successfully executed codes by the total number of codes written. This reward encourages the model to write more code and ensure its correctness.
> - **Process reward:** The entire thinking process is fed into Qwen2.5-VL-72B, which assigns a score based on the text quality and reasoning logic.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 10[CN]:</strong></span>
>
> - **一致性奖励（Consistency reward）：** 我们向 Qwen2.5-VL-72B 提供思考过程的最后 500 个字符（通常是一段总结）以及答案内容，请其评估推理与最终答案之间的一致性。
> - **代码奖励（Code reward）：** 该奖励是模型生成代码中成功运行的比例，通过成功执行的代码数量除以所编写代码的总数计算得出。该奖励鼓励模型编写更多代码并确保其正确性。
> - **过程奖励（Process reward）：** 将完整的思考过程输入 Qwen2.5-VL-72B，后者根据文本质量和推理逻辑给出分数。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 13:</strong></span> Figure 7 illustrates the dynamics of several critical metrics during the RL process, from which we derive the following observations and findings.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 13[CN]:</strong></span> Figure 7 展示了 RL 过程中若干关键指标的动态变化，我们据此得到以下观察与发现。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 14:</strong></span> First, the average response length shows a rapid initial decrease before gradually converging. However, the images in this dataset are often not complex, rendering many of the coding operations non-essential. During the RL phase, the model quickly discerns that for a majority of tasks, a direct textual response is more efficient than a code-based analysis. Consequently, there is a swift reduction in the generation of unnecessary code. Second, the result reward exhibits a generally upward trend, which aligns with our expectations for a successful learning process. A more noteworthy observation pertains to the consistency reward. In the initial training stages, the average result reward is approximately 0.5, whereas the average consistency reward is only 0.15. This disparity indicates that a significant number of initially correct answers lack coherent reasoning, highlighting the critical role of the consistency reward. By incorporating this reward, the model is incentivized to align its reasoning with its final output. This demonstrates that the inclusion of the consistency reward substantially mitigates the problem of contradictions between the model’s reasoning and its answers.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 14[CN]:</strong></span> 首先，平均响应长度起初迅速下降，随后逐渐收敛。这一现象源于 SFT 阶段：该阶段引入大量合成生成的图像操作数据，以教会模型通过生成代码解决问题。然而，该数据集中的图像往往并不复杂，使得许多编码操作并非必要。在 RL 阶段，模型很快意识到，对于大多数任务，直接给出文本响应比基于代码的分析更高效。因此，不必要代码的生成迅速减少。其次，结果奖励总体呈上升趋势，这符合我们对成功学习过程的预期。更值得注意的观察与一致性奖励有关。在训练初期，平均结果奖励约为 0.5，而平均一致性奖励仅为 0.15。这一差距表明，大量起初正确的答案缺乏连贯推理，突显了一致性奖励的关键作用。通过引入该奖励，模型受到激励，使其推理与最终输出保持一致。随着训练推进，结果奖励收敛到约 0.7，而一致性奖励上升到接近 0.35。这表明，加入一致性奖励显著缓解了模型推理与答案相互矛盾的问题。

## 6 Conclusion and Limitations

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 1:</strong></span> We introduce Thyme, a novel paradigm that empowers MLLMs to autonomously generate and execute code for a wide range of image manipulations and complex computations. Our approach utilizes a two-stage training strategy, beginning with a comprehensive SFT phase on a curated 500K-sample dataset to instill foundational coding abilities for tasks like cropping, rotation, and mathematical calculations. This is followed by a RL phase, where we employ our proposed GRPO-ATS algorithm to refine the model’s decision-making and executional precision. GRPO-ATS adaptively uses different sampling temperatures for text and code generation, which effectively balances creative reasoning with the need for accurate, executable code. Extensive evaluations across nearly 20 benchmarks demonstrate that Thyme achieves significant and consistent performance improvements over the baseline, particularly in challenging high-resolution perception and complex reasoning tasks.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 1[CN]:</strong></span> 我们提出了 Thyme，这是一种新颖范式，使多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）能够自主生成并执行代码，以完成广泛的图像操作与复杂计算。我们的方法采用两阶段训练策略：首先，在精心构建的 500K 样本数据集上进行全面的监督微调（SFT），为裁剪、旋转和数学计算等任务赋予基础编码能力；随后进入强化学习（RL）阶段，采用我们提出的 GRPO-ATS 算法来优化模型的决策能力与执行精度。GRPO-ATS 自适应地为文本生成和代码生成采用不同的采样温度，从而有效平衡创造性推理与准确、可执行代码之间的需求。在近 20 个基准上的广泛评估表明，Thyme 相较基线取得了显著且一致的性能提升，尤其是在具有挑战性的高分辨率感知与复杂推理任务上。

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 2:</strong></span> Despite the promising results, our work has several limitations that open avenues for future research:

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 2[CN]:</strong></span> 尽管取得了令人鼓舞的结果，我们的工作仍存在若干局限，也由此为未来研究开辟了方向：

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 3:</strong></span>
>
> - **Model Capability.** The performance of Thymeis inherently constrained by the capabilities of its base model. The current model’s proficiency in precise object localization and sophisticated code generation is limited. This can occasionally lead to the generation of incorrect cropping operations or non-standard, unexecutable code. We believe that leveraging more powerful foundation models (i.e., a stronger base model) could significantly mitigate these issues, leading to more reliable and accurate tool use.
> - **Evaluation Scope.** Our evaluation is limited by the nature of existing benchmarks. The majority of image data in widely-used benchmarks consists of high-quality, well-oriented images from everyday scenarios. Consequently, there is a lack of robust numerical evaluation for certain image manipulation capabilities that Thyme enables, such as correcting for image rotation or enhancing low-contrast images, as these scenarios are underrepresented. The development of new benchmarks specifically designed to assess these advanced image processing operations would be invaluable for a more comprehensive evaluation of models like Thyme.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 3[CN]:</strong></span>
>
> - **模型能力。** Thyme 的性能从根本上受其基础模型能力的约束。当前模型在精确目标定位和复杂代码生成方面的能力有限，这有时会导致生成错误的裁剪操作，或生成不规范、不可执行的代码。我们认为，采用更强大的基础模型（即更强的基座模型）可以显著缓解这些问题，从而实现更加可靠、准确的工具使用。
> - **评估范围。** 我们的评估受现有基准性质的限制。广泛使用的基准中，大多数图像数据都是来自日常场景、质量较高且方向正常的图像。因此，对于 Thyme 所支持的某些图像操作能力，例如校正图像旋转或增强低对比度图像，目前缺乏稳健的定量评估，因为这些场景在现有基准中的代表性不足。开发专门用于评估这些高级图像处理操作的新基准，对于更加全面地评估 Thyme 这类模型将极具价值。

## B Related Work

> <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 1:</strong></span> **Multimodal Large Language Models.** Fueled by the advancements in LLMs the field of MLLMs has seen rapid development in recent years, with model capabilities evolving at a remarkable pace Fu et al. (2024b); Zhang et al. (2024d); Yu et al. (2025); Li et al. (2022). Modern MLLMs, such as Qwen2.5-VL Bai et al. (2025a), GPT-4o OpenAI. (2024), and LLaVA Li et al. (2024a), have demonstrated impressive performance in processing high-resolution images and engaging in complex human-like dialogue. Research has diversified into numerous sub-domains, including extending context length Shen et al. (2025b), improving computational efficiency Zhang et al. (2024a; 2025e), mitigating hallucinations Lu et al. (2025); Zhang et al. (2024e), enhancing conversational abilities Xiong et al. (2024), and achieving better alignment with human preferences Zhang et al. (2025c). Concurrently, more sophisticated architectures have emerged. Omni-MLLMs are capable of processing a mix of modalities like speech, video, and images simultaneously Li et al. (2025); Zhao et al. (2025); Fu et al. (2024c), while Unify-MLLMs can produce interleaved, mixed-modal outputs, such as generating an image with auxiliary lines to aid in solving a math problem Xie et al. (2024); Team (2024). These works showcase a clear trajectory towards more integrated and versatile multimodal interaction.

> <span style="color:#F59E0B"><strong>Para. 1[CN]:</strong></span> **多模态大语言模型。** 在大语言模型（LLMs）进步的推动下，多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）领域近年来迅速发展，模型能力以惊人的速度演进 Fu et al. (2024b); Zhang et al. (2024d); Yu et al. (2025); Li et al. (2022)。Qwen2.5-VL Bai et al. (2025a)、GPT-4o OpenAI. (2024) 和 LLaVA Li et al. (2024a) 等现代 MLLMs，在处理高分辨率图像以及开展复杂、类人对话方面展现出令人瞩目的性能。相关研究已经扩展到众多子领域，包括扩展上下文长度 Shen et al. (2025b)、提升计算效率 Zhang et al. (2024a; 2025e)、缓解幻觉 Lu et al. (2025); Zhang et al. (2024e)、增强对话能力 Xiong et al. (2024)，以及实现与人类偏好的更好对齐 Zhang et al. (2025c)。与此同时，更复杂的架构也不断涌现。Omni-MLLMs 能够同时处理语音、视频和图像等多种模态的混合输入 Li et al. (2025); Zhao et al. (2025); Fu et al. (2024c)，而 Unify-MLLMs 则能够生成交错的混合模态输出，例如生成带有辅助线的图像，以帮助求解数学问题 Xie et al. > <span style="color:#3B82F6"><strong>Para. 2:</strong></span> **Multimodal Reasoning.** Enhancing the reasoning capabilities of MLLMs is a critical frontier. Recently, RL has become a prominent technique in the post-training of MLLMs, leading to significant gains in vision tasks Liu et al. (2025); Shen et al. (2025c), multimodal reasoning Huang et al. (2025b); Peng et al. (2025); Meng et al. (2025), and even reward modeling itself Zhang et al. (2025d). Compared to traditional methods like SFT or Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) Rafailov et al. (2023), RL-based approaches have shown superior generalization and an ability to induce more complex, long-term reasoning capabilities, as demonstrated by models like DeepSeek-R1 DeepSeek-AI (2025). However, a significant limitation of many existing efforts is that they primarily focus on enhancing the textual reasoning chain. The visual input often serves as a static condition rather than an active component within the reasoning process. While some paradigms have emerged to “think with images”, they are often limited to a single function like cropping Zheng et al. (2025) or generating an auxiliary image Chern et al. (2025). Our work, Thyme, directly addresses this gap by empowering the model to autonomously generate and execute code for a diverse range of image manipulations and computations. This allows the model to treat the image not just as input, but as a dynamic entity that can be actively interrogated and transformed as an integral part of its reasoning process.
